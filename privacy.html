Privacy policy

Personal Mail Monitor is a personal-use integration operated by the owner of autoairob@gmail.com. This policy distinguishes the public informational website from the private automation.

This website

These static pages contain no login forms, tracking scripts, advertising or embedded third-party resources. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process connection information and access logs under GitHub's privacy statement. Emailing the contact address shares your message and address with the operator and their email provider.

Google account access

The private automation uses Google authorization to retrieve and process Capital One-related email for personal monitoring. Depending on the authorization granted, account permissions may include reading, sending and modifying Gmail messages and access to Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Sheets and Docs. Granted permissions are shown on Google's consent screen; permission to access a service does not mean this website publishes its data.

Processing and disclosure

Account data and derived results are handled separately from this public website, through the owner's automation environment. Connected automation, notification and AI services may process information when the owner uses them. Do not assume that all processing is local or that notifications remain on the owner's server. This site does not offer account access to visitors.

Storage and deletion

OAuth credentials, automation state, results, logs and notification copies may persist in the systems involved. Retention depends on those systems' configuration; this policy does not promise a fixed automatic deletion period. Contact the operator to request a review of stored information, connected processors or deletion. Copies held by service providers may be subject to their separate retention policies.

Revoke access

Review or revoke Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized access using that grant; it does not automatically remove previously stored records or notification copies.
